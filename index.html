Vibe Coding in GIS

A Handbook for Designers

Haozhuo Yang

Harvard Graduate School of Design

This handbook introduces vibe coding as a method for spatial analysis — describing operations in natural language and letting AI generate the code. It covers spatial analysis workflows, expressive cartography, and building reusable tools, with hands-on workshops using ArcGIS Pro.

Based on "Experimental Cartography: Vibe Coding for GIS," a J-Term course taught at Harvard GSD in January 2026.
